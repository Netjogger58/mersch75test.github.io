# Luxembourgish Language Guide

Use this note whenever Luxembourgish copy is written or revised for this project.

## Mandatory references

Luxembourgish output must follow these official sources only:

- LOD (Letzebuerger Online Dictionnaire): https://lod.lu
- ZLS (Zenter fir d'Letzebuerger Sprooch)
- Orthografie-Reglement 2019
- spellchecker.lu / Schreifmaschinn.lu as tools aligned with the official standards

Do not rely on informal, dialectal, outdated, or inferred spellings when official Luxembourgish wording is needed.

## Recommended prompt instruction

"Generiere den folgenden Text ausschliesslich in luxemburgischer Sprache. Als alleinige Basis fuer Grammatik, Syntax und Rechtschreibung gilt das Regelwerk des 'Zenter fir d'Letzebuerger Sprooch' (ZLS) sowie der 'Letzebuerger Online Dictionnaire' (lod.lu). Wende zwingend die offiziellen Rechtschreibregeln des Orthografie-Reglements von 2019 an. Ueberpruefe den Output gedanklich gegen die Datenbanken von lod.lu, bevor der Text ausgegeben wird."

## Extra grammar directive

Always enforce this additional rule explicitly:

"Achte bei der Generierung explizit und strikt auf die korrekte und lueckenlose Anwendung der Eifeler Regel (Eifeler Regel / Wegfall des 'n' oder 'nn' am Wortende je nach folgendem Konsonanten)."

## Working rule for this repo

For all future Luxembourgish website text in this project:

- prefer official Luxembourgish standard forms
- check spelling against LOD-style conventions
- apply the Eifeler Regel consistently
- avoid German, French, or dialect interference unless the original wording requires it

## Sports vocabulary exception

In sports-related copy, some terms may not exist in the official Luxembourgish dictionaries or may sound distorted if they are translated too aggressively.

Use this rule:

- prefer an official Luxembourgish form when one clearly exists
- keep established German, French, or English sports terms when no official standard term is available
- also keep the loanword when replacing it would distort the sporting meaning, role, tactic, competition name, or common usage
- do not force artificial Luxembourgish wording at the expense of clarity or accuracy
